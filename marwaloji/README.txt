MARWA LO G — Premium biscuit brand website
==============================================

FILES
- index.html       Main website
- style.css        Design, responsive layout, animations and 3D-style packet
- script.js        Packet movement, front/back controls, mobile menu, contact email
- assets/          Your uploaded front and back packet photos

RUN LOCALLY
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Keep index.html, style.css, script.js and the assets folder together.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome.
4. To edit, open the extracted folder in VS Code.

INTERACTIVE PACKET
- Move your mouse over the packet to tilt it.
- Click and drag or touch and drag to rotate it.
- Use View front / View back / Reset buttons.
- Keyboard users can focus the packet and use arrow keys.
- This is a CSS 3D-style presentation built from your front/back photos, not a fully modelled 3D scan. A real 3D model with accurate thickness and side textures would require a GLB/GLTF model.

CONTACT
- The contact email button opens a draft in the visitor's email application. It does not send automatically.
- The quick message form also opens a pre-filled email draft. For messages to be stored/submitted directly on a server, a backend or form service must be connected.

BEFORE PUBLIC LAUNCH
- Verify the final food label, ingredient legality (including shilajit extract), nutrition values, FSSAI licence details, manufacturer details and product claims with a qualified food compliance professional.
- The provided package images are displayed as supplied; their printed claims have not been independently verified.
- Only use slogans and marketing claims that are appropriate, truthful and compliant.
- This site is a front-end prototype. It does not process payments or take online orders yet.
